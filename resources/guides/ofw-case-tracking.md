# OFW Case Tracking Guide — One Window Bayanihan

> **Purpose:** Help OFWs and family members follow a case without exposing someone else's information.
> **Updated:** 2026-11-01

## What is the Bayanihan One Window System?

- **Signed-in citizen portal:** sign in to see cases associated with your client profile, their timelines, agency updates, and notifications. Register and verify your email before filing a new request.
- **Public tracker:** open `/track`, enter your tracker number and matching client email, then verify an emailed code. You do not need to sign in to an account for this path.

The system is operated by the **Department of Migrant Workers (DMW)** Region VII and routes your request to the partner agency that can act on it.

New requests wait for Case Manager review. They can appear while still under review; you do not need to wait for approval to follow a client-visible filing.

## How to Check Case Status Using a Tracker Number

1. Open the public case tracking page.
2. Copy your **tracker number** from your case record or intake review email. Newly generated numbers use `OWBAP-` followed by ten characters, for example `OWBAP-0123456789`. Copy your issued number exactly; older issued numbers may have a different format. The internal case number beginning `OWB-` is not the public tracker number.
3. Enter the email associated with the case's client record.
4. Request a verification code. If the tracker and email match a client-visible case, the system sends a six-digit code to that email.
5. Enter the code to open the case view.

Email comparison ignores letter case and leading/trailing spaces. It does not ignore spelling differences, changed dots, or a different domain. An unknown tracker or mismatched email returns a generic error; the system does **not** send a code to an arbitrary wrong address.

## How the OTP Verification Process Works

- Codes expire after **five minutes** and can be used once.
- A new code replaces the previous code. Use the newest email.
- Requests and verification attempts are limited. If temporarily blocked, wait before trying again; resends are not unlimited.
- A verified public tracking session lasts at most **30 minutes** and also depends on the browser session. Verify again if access expires.
- Do not share your code. A support conversation should not require you to disclose it.

## What Different Case Statuses Mean

The case view presents a plain-language case summary, a timeline, and separate agency referral updates. The latest 200 case events are retained for this view and shown in chronological order; this is not an unlimited archive of all historical events.

| Case wording | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Being reviewed** | The filing is waiting for or undergoing Case Manager review. |
| **In progress** | The case is open. Partner-office referrals may be underway; if none exist yet, the page explains that referrals will appear when sent. |
| **Resolved** | Staff have closed the case. |
| **Archived** | The case has been archived. |

Each agency has its own referral status, separate from the overall case:

| Agency referral | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Pending** | Waiting for the receiving agency to act. |
| **Processing** | The agency is working on the referral. |
| **For compliance / waiting on you** | The agency needs further information or documents. Read its request and instructions. |
| **Completed** | The agency has finished its work on this referral. |
| **Rejected / unable to assist** | The agency could not take or complete this referral. This does not necessarily mean the whole case is closed. |

Approval opens a case for casework; it is not a guarantee that a benefit or particular service has been granted. A rejected filing is a separate staff review outcome; check your notifications/email and contact DMW if you need help understanding the reason.

## What Information You Need

An agency may email a private request link or make a request available through your authorized portal view. Open it to read the checklist, reply, and submit requested files. Private links expire and can be revoked or replaced. If access fails, ask the agency for a fresh link rather than forwarding someone else's link.

Request status is separate from case and referral status: a request may be awaiting your response, being reviewed, response sent, completed, or cancelled. Finished referrals and closed/archived cases restrict further client-facing activity.

## Troubleshooting Tips

### Lost tracker number

Check your intake review email, case record, and citizen portal. If you cannot find the number, contact DMW Region VII through the application's current contact page. Provide enough information for staff to identify the filing securely; do not post personal information publicly.

### Wrong email or generic error

Check both fields for typing errors. Use the case client's email, which may differ from a family member's account email. If you no longer control that address, contact DMW for identity verification and correction. Repeated guessing will not bypass verification.

### Code expired or not arriving

Check Spam/Junk, allow time for delivery, and request a fresh code when the request limit permits. Use only the latest code. Delivery can be delayed; the application does not guarantee arrival within seconds. Contact the office if the issue persists.

### Case has not changed

Check agency milestones and your email for follow-up requests. Contact the handling office using the current partner directory or contact page. Processing time depends on the agency and request; this guide does not promise a fixed turnaround or a 15-working-day escalation rule.

## Data Privacy Reminder

Keep tracker numbers, verification codes, private links, and case screenshots confidential. Use official contact channels shown in the application. Never disclose passwords, recovery codes, or email verification codes to someone claiming to help you track a case.
